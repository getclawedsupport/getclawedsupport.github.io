GET CLAWED

Crab riders on a tiny ocean world. The higher rider wins.

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email getclawedsupport@proton.me.

Free plays

A play is one game of 3 lives. Everyone starts with 10 starter plays to use whenever they like. After those, you get 3 free plays a day, which refill at midnight.

Unlimited Play Pass

A one-time purchase that removes the limit for good. It isn't a subscription. To get it on another device, or after reinstalling, open the pass offer in the game and tap Restore purchase while signed in to the same Apple Account.

Playing offline

The game plays fully offline. Only buying or restoring the pass needs a connection.

Privacy

Get Clawed collects no data. Read the privacy policy.
