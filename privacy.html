Get Clawed Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

Get Clawed is a game by Jadine Yee. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

The short version

Get Clawed does not collect, store or share any personal information. Nothing you do in the game is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

The game keeps a few things on your device so it works:

  • your high scores and the initials you enter with them;
  • how many free games you've played;
  • whether you own the Unlimited Play Pass;
  • a random number, made when you first open the game, that sets which price you're offered.

None of this is ever sent to us. Deleting the app deletes your high scores. The free-game count and the random number are kept in your device's keychain, so reinstalling the game doesn't reset them. If you use iCloud Keychain, Apple syncs the random number between your own devices, so you're offered the same price on each.

Motion sensor

If you tilt your device to sidestep, the game reads the motion sensor while you play. The readings are used in the moment and are never stored or sent anywhere.

Purchases

Apple handles all purchases through the App Store. We never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. Apple's privacy policy covers those purchases: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

No ads, no tracking, no accounts

The game shows no ads and includes no analytics or advertising software. It does not track you across other apps or websites. You don't need an account to play.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
